MC68331 clock pins: pins 51–83

Oncore VP 8-channel. Pin numbers from Motorola's MC68331 technical data, Figure 2 (132-pin QFP). Pin 1 is at the middle of the top edge; numbers run counterclockwise, so this bottom edge reads 51 at the left to 83 at the right.

What to measure, with the CPU halted at reset

  1. Pin 62, EXTAL: frequency and waveform, with a 10x probe. Expect about 29.1 kHz (7.4505 MHz ÷ 256). A full logic-level square wave means another chip drives it; a small sine-like signal means a crystal right at the CPU.
  2. Pin 60, XTAL: oscillating, or flat? Oscillating means a crystal sits across pins 60 and 62. Flat or unconnected means EXTAL is driven from outside.
  3. Pin 78, MODCLK: DC level. High means PLL mode, which the 7.45 MHz reading predicts.
  4. If EXTAL is a square wave: probe the MC68HC68T1's CLK OUT (pin 1) for the same 29.1 kHz. If it doesn't match, follow pin 62's trace back to its source.

Pins 61–66 sit between grounds and supplies; probe carefully, since a slip onto VDDSYN or XFC can upset the PLL. Pin 82 is AS and pin 83 is VSS; ADDR5 is pin 24 on this package.
